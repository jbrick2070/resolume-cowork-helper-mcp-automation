# The Manual - run sheet

## Before the first frame

1. Install: `APC 40 MK II - The Manual.xml` -> `Documents\Resolume Avenue\Shortcuts\MIDI\`; `The Manual - Avenue 7.28.1.avc` -> `Documents\Resolume Avenue\Compositions\` (this rig's Documents is under OneDrive).
2. **Audio in.** Preferences -> Audio -> Input device: `CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable)`. Play your music into `CABLE Input`. The composition uses External FFT; with no input device every reactive parameter sits at its floor and the picture looks static.
3. Preferences -> MIDI: `APC40 mkII` as input AND output (output drives the pad colours).
4. Open the composition. It selects its own MIDI preset.
5. Park the CROSSFADER in the centre and all eight faders up.

## Opening

* **PLAY** fires scene 1 of the current deck and arms the SCOPE.
* Watch the SCOPE (layer 8, fader 8): if it is flat, the audio input is not set.
* Track knobs 1-6 are depth / release for the three bands; turn depth up until the SCOPE breathes with the music, not with every hi-hat.

## Playing

* Scenes with Scene Launch 1-5 and UP/DOWN; worlds with LEFT/RIGHT.
* Pads mash the five playable layers across any scene.
* Effects: lit button = active. Device knobs 1-4 are the four banks' wetness, 5-8 the macros. CUE LEVEL sweeps all four banks.
* **STOP** clears the pad layers only (beds and scope survive). **STOP ALL CLIPS** ejects everything. **RECORD** is blackout (press again to return). The footswitch is a momentary blackout.
* **BANK LOCK = CLEAN.** Every effect dry, every macro off, picture untouched. Avenue has no soft-takeover: after CLEAN the physical knobs are where you left them, so the next knob you touch jumps. Sweep CUE LEVEL to come back smoothly.

## Signature moments, deck by deck

**Vocabulary**
- Hierarchy: walk DOWN from Composition to Column one per bar with nothing lit; the blue frame shrinks to a tab, a strip, a bracket, a cell, a column.
- Clip: on column 5 tap the row-2 BODY pad on the beat so the green thumbnail retriggers.
- Render order: column 8, Device knob 6 (ECHO) at a third so the green pass leaves a trace down the pipeline rows.

**The Clip**
- Piano: Scene Launch 1, right-click the row-2 BODY clip > Trigger style > Piano, then play the pad like a key - the green cell exists only while you hold it. That is Piano.
- Beat snap: Composition > Beat Snap > 1 Bar, then Scene Launch 2 and tap Scene Launch 3 off the beat; Avenue waits for the bar. Watch the amber metronome.
- Fader start: column 5, turn Layer > Fader Start on for layer 3, then ride fader 3 down and up on the phrase; the green restart line jumps each time.

**Parameters**
- FFT: Scene Launch 4 and turn Track knob 3 (MID DEPTH) up until the whole picture blooms on the mids; the spectrum in column 4 already rides the signal on its own. This scene is the pack explaining its own reactivity.
- Envelope: column 6, Device knob 2 (TEXTURE) at a quarter with GOUACHE lit so the elastic curve smears as it overshoots.
- Dashboard: column 7, turn Device knobs 1-4 and watch the dials - they are the real links.

**Blend Modes**
- A over B: on any column, ride faders 2 and 3 against each other; the discs overlap at centre.
- Lighten: Scene Launch 2 with Device knob 5 (GLOW) up so Screen actually screens.
- Transitions: column 8, Track knob 8 (TIME) past three-quarters so the wipe and push race.

**This Rig**
- Audio in: Scene Launch 1 - if the input meter is flat, Preferences > Audio is not set to CABLE Output. The composition explains its own silence.
- MIDI: column 2, press any pad; the pack's LED feedback is what Preferences > MIDI output is for.
- Shortcut mode: DOWN to column 8 on the last bar, every fader up, and let the interface go blue; BLACKOUT to leave.
