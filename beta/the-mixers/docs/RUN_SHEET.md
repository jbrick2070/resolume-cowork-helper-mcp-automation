# The Mixers - run sheet

## Before the first frame

1. Install: `APC 40 MK II - The Mixers.xml` -> `Documents\Resolume Avenue\Shortcuts\MIDI\`; `The Mixers - V-4, MXPro, MX-1 + JVC.avc` -> `Documents\Resolume Avenue\Compositions\` (this rig's Documents is under OneDrive).
2. **Audio in.** Preferences -> Audio -> Input device: `CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable)`. Play your music into `CABLE Input`. The composition uses External FFT; with no input device every reactive parameter sits at its floor and the picture looks static.
3. Preferences -> MIDI: `APC40 mkII` as input AND output (output drives the pad colours).
4. Open the composition. It selects its own MIDI preset.
5. Park the CROSSFADER in the centre and all eight faders up.

## Opening

* **PLAY** fires scene 1 of the current deck and arms the SCOPE.
* Watch the SCOPE (layer 8, fader 8): if it is flat, the audio input is not set.
* Track knobs 1-6 are depth / release for the three bands; turn depth up until the SCOPE breathes with the music, not with every hi-hat.

## Playing

* Scenes with Scene Launch 1-5 and UP/DOWN; worlds with LEFT/RIGHT.
* Pads mash the five playable layers across any scene.
* Effects: lit button = active. Device knobs 1-4 are the four banks' wetness, 5-8 the macros. CUE LEVEL sweeps all four banks.
* **STOP** clears the pad layers only (beds and scope survive). **STOP ALL CLIPS** ejects everything. **RECORD** is blackout (press again to return). The footswitch is a momentary blackout.
* **BANK LOCK = CLEAN.** Every effect dry, every macro off, picture untouched. Avenue has no soft-takeover: after CLEAN the physical knobs are where you left them, so the next knob you touch jumps. Sweep CUE LEVEL to come back smoothly.

## Signature moments, deck by deck

**APC40 mkII**
- Column 8 first: read the legend, then press RIGHT once; the V-4 appears. RIGHT again, the MXPro. LEFT walks back. That is the whole navigation.
- Column 5 with the real crossfader: the amber bar on screen follows the lows, your hand follows it.
- Column 2, any pad: note 0 lights bottom-left on screen and on the desk at once.

**Edirol V-4**
- BPM Sync on the downbeat: tap TAP TEMPO on the APC with the music; the green pulse is the V-4 cutting for you.
- T-Bar with the real crossfader, slowly end to end.
- MIDI then Output Fade: two columns DOWN - the wire, then the fade to white.

**Videonics MXPro**
- 501 then Shapes on consecutive downbeats: the number, then the keyhole.
- Keypad + Play: press the APC's PLAY as the MXPro's PLAY.
- Learn last, then RIGHT: from the MXPro back to where it started, the MX-1.

**Videonics MX-1**
- Take-Bar with the real crossfader: the gesture the whole pack is built on, from 1994.
- Film Look under a slow track: the strobe at setting 1.
- The Club last: the manual's own nightclub, on the APC that replaced it.

**JVC S-VHS + Edit**
- Jog then Shuttle under a breakdown: one frame, then seven times speed.
- Heads on a heavy kick: the drum turns with the lows.
- Perform last: the red key, then RIGHT to the Patch Bay.

**Patch Bay**
- Column 2 and the real crossfader: the gesture every mixer here was built around.
- Walk columns 4 to 7 DOWN on consecutive downbeats: dissolve, wipe, key, freeze - the grammar.
- Column 8 last, then LEFT four times back to the APC40 deck: the loop closes.
