# JVC RM-G850E (and the HR-S7600U): sourced facts

Gathered 2026-10-23 for the JVC deck. Every caption on that deck comes from a line here.

## The headline: these two never worked together
The RM-G850E is not the HR-S7600U's remote. It is a professional, mains-powered, wired, two-machine
(player + recorder) **editing control unit** for JVC's professional VTRs of the U-matic CR-850U /
CR-600U era, connected by 9-pin serial. No source links it to any HR-S consumer S-VHS deck,
to infrared, or to JLIP.

- E = European/PAL, U = USA/NTSC, as on the sister RM-G800E / RM-G800U https://www.globalmediapro.com/dp/A00NK9/JVC-RM-G800E-Cuts-Editing-Controller/
- Release year, dimensions, weight: not found (late 1980s inferred from the CR-850U pairing only)

### Panel, read from the 2019 video (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=koXSPcx6_PU)
- Two large search / jog dials, one PLAYER, one RECORDER, each scaled STILL at the top and x1 to the right, with a MONITOR slider beside each
- PLAYER: REW, FF, STOP, PLAY, STILL, SEARCH, JOG. RECORDER: REC (red), REW, FF, STOP, PLAY, PAUSE/STILL
- Edit-mode keys, colour-coded: ASSEM (orange), AUDIO 1 and AUDIO 2 (green), VIDEO (blue), REC-EE (white)
- Memory: player trims +10S / -1S, recorder trims +10F / -1F, IN and OUT for each; CANCEL, ENTRY
- EDITING: PREVIEW, PERFORM (red), STOP, GO TO, REVIEW
- No number keypad, no timer keys

### Its deck, the CR-850U
- JVC's 3/4-inch U-matic editing recorder, "an answer to the Sony BVU series"; serial RS-422 and parallel edit connectors; jog mode with a serial controller; search 1/30x to 10x, high speed 15x forward / 20x reverse https://bibbteck.com/en/jvc-cr850u-umatic-editing-recorder

## HR-S7600U
Sources: [MAN] JVC instruction manual LPT0240-001A, 76 pp, footers dated 99.4.28 https://web.archive.org/web/20230216074626/http://resources.jvc.com/Resources/00/00/99/LPT0240-001A.pdf (also https://archive.org/details/manual_HRS7600U_OM_JVC) · [JVCAM] https://jvc-america.com/vcrs/hr_s7600u.html · [JVCSUP] https://support.jvc.com/consumer/product.jsp?modelId=MODL022115 · [AR] http://www.audioreview.com/product/home-video/vcrs/jvc/hr-s7600u.html · [LINE] https://jvc-america.com/vcrs/super_vhs_content.html

- A 1999 model: manual printed 28 Apr 1999 [MAN p.1]; owners had them by October 1999 [AR]
- US/NTSC; the US unit came in black only (digitalFAQ forum, 2017)
- S-VHS / VHS, 437 x 97 x 347 mm, 4.8 kg [MAN p.73]
- MSRP $449.95 [JVCAM]; JVC's S-VHS line: HR-S9600U $649.95 > HR-S7600U $449.95 > HR-S4600U > HR-S3600U [LINE]
- "Dual Azimuth - 4 Video Heads + 2 Hi-Fi Audio Heads + Flying Erase Head" [JVCSUP]; "DA-4 (Double Azimuth) head helical scan system"; 400 lines in S-VHS, 240 in VHS [MAN p.73]
- Hi-Fi stereo, 20 Hz - 20 kHz [MAN p.73]
- S-VHS ET: "record with S-VHS picture quality on VHS cassettes" [MAN p.42]
- JOG dial and SHUTTLE ring on the FRONT PANEL [MAN p.70][JVCAM]
- JOG: "Turn the JOG dial to the right for forward frame-by-frame playback, or to the left for reverse" [MAN p.24]
- SHUTTLE: TimeScan search up to 7x either way; "It remains at that position even after you release it" [MAN p.34-35]
- Pro-Slow: smooth slow motion, forward and reverse [MAN p.24]
- Digital TBC with linked Digital 3-DNR; JVC recommends it "when... using this VCR as the player for editing" [MAN p.37]; "DigiPure Technology with 2MB Frame Memory" [JVCAM]
- Insert editing and audio dubbing [MAN p.58, p.60]
- Rear: S-Video and composite in/out, RF, and a REMOTE PAUSE / AV COMPU-LINK 3.5 mm jack a JVC camcorder can drive [MAN p.56-57, p.71]; no JLIP / J-Terminal (that is the HR-S9600U) [MAN][JVCAM]
- Supplied remote: "MULTI BRAND REMOTE CONTROL UNIT", infrared, no jog/shuttle; JVC calls its rocker "Shuttle Plus" [MAN p.72][JVCSUP]
- Still picture stops after 5 minutes "to protect the heads" [MAN p.68]
- Quotable: "This is the Ferrari of VCR design." (owner review, Mar 2000) [AR]
