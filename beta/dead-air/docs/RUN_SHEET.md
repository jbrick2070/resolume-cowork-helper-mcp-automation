# Dead Air - run sheet

## Before the first frame

1. Install: `APC 40 MK II - Dead Air.xml` -> `Documents\Resolume Avenue\Shortcuts\MIDI\`; `Dead Air Listening Posts.avc` -> `Documents\Resolume Avenue\Compositions\` (this rig's Documents is under OneDrive).
2. **Audio in.** Preferences -> Audio -> Input device: `CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable)`. Play your music into `CABLE Input`. The composition uses External FFT; with no input device every reactive parameter sits at its floor and the picture looks static.
3. Preferences -> MIDI: `APC40 mkII` as input AND output (output drives the pad colours).
4. Open the composition. It selects its own MIDI preset.
5. Park the CROSSFADER in the centre and all eight faders up.

## Opening

* **PLAY** fires scene 1 of the current deck and arms the SCOPE.
* Watch the SCOPE (layer 8, fader 8): if it is flat, the audio input is not set.
* Track knobs 1-6 are depth / release for the three bands; turn depth up until the SCOPE breathes with the music, not with every hi-hat.
* **FFT GAIN is the TEMPO knob**, on every composition: Avenue's global audio gain. Turn it up when the picture is too calm for the room, down when it slams. METRONOME puts it back to the middle.
* **TIME is Track knob 8**: composition speed, 0 to 2x with 1x at the centre. TAP TEMPO puts it back to exactly 1x.

## Playing

* Scenes with Scene Launch 1-5 and UP/DOWN; worlds with LEFT/RIGHT.
* Pads mash the five playable layers across any scene.
* Effects: lit button = active. Device knobs 1-4 are the four banks' wetness, 5-8 the macros. CUE LEVEL nudges all four banks together (anticlockwise toward the clean picture).
* **STOP** clears the pad layers only (beds and scope survive). **STOP ALL CLIPS** ejects everything. **RECORD** is blackout (press again to return). The footswitch is a momentary blackout.
* **BANK LOCK = CLEAN.** Every effect dry, every macro off, picture untouched. Avenue has no soft-takeover: after CLEAN the physical knobs are where you left them, so the next knob you touch jumps. Sweep CUE LEVEL to come back smoothly.

## Signature moments, deck by deck

**Hydrogen Line**
- CP 1919 stack: fire Scene Launch 4, hold the row-4 LIGHT pad (Piano trigger) so the 1.337-second Stroboscope flashes under the React Pulsar ridgeline on row 2, roll the bank-A knob to half with only Trails lit so each flash settles into the stacked plot, then roll it back to zero and the scruff is just ink again.
- Wow Signal: fire Scene Launch 5 and tap the row-5 TEXT pad on the downbeat to retrigger the 6EQUJ5 typewriter in the deck's only red while the row-4 Gradient rises over its seventy-two seconds, hit the Freeze button on the same beat if the room can take the hold, and on the drop tap Invert RGB for one bar so the paper goes black and the ink goes white.
- Exposure to dot: walk Up to Deep Field, fire the row-1 BED pad with the bank-A knob at three-quarters and only Trails lit so Dot Dot Dot accumulates like a plate developing for a full minute, press the Trails button off and on to wipe the plate, then pull faders 1, 2 and 4 to zero, tap the row-3 FIGURE pad for the one-pixel Shaper under LOOK AGAIN AT THAT DOT, and ride the bank-A knob with only Vignette lit until the dot is the only lit thing in the club for sixteen bars.

**Loss Of Signal**
- LOS In Ten to Dead Air: fire Scene Launch 3 and tap the row-5 TEXT pad on the downbeat so the count types 10 to 1 one digit a beat, then fire Scene Launch 4 so Dead Air lands with only BED and SCOPE, and let the room hear the silence for a full phrase before you touch a pad.
- Heartbeat: still in Dead Air, press the row-3 FIGURE pad and the row-4 LIGHT pad together so the one Metaball swells on the sub band under the Heartbeat strobe, press row 2 for the flat ECG above it, and on the breakdown hit the Freeze button, whose Y split is set at the half in the build, so the heart's half of the frame holds while the ECG keeps scrolling flat.
- Alarm to sunrise: fire Scene Launch 5 (Whistling) and turn the bank-B knob up with Tunnel lit so the corridor pulls inward under the violet, walk Up once to Alarm 1202 and hit Strobe on the downbeat with Shift Glitch tapped on the hits and the bank-C knob at half for CRT, then walk Up once more to Acquisition and tap Shift RGB on the long white flash only, releasing before it fades, so the sunrise splits into three and the room is clean again.

**The Sitters**
- Knock Once: fire Scene Launch 3 and answer the kick by hand, one press of the row-4 LIGHT pad per knock and never more than once a bar, so the knocks arrive like answers and not like a strobe, while the row-5 text reads KNOCK ONCE FOR YES above the spaced Y E S.
- Moth To Lamp: fire Scene Launch 5, tap the row-3 FIGURE pad on the one of every bar to re-spawn the moths around the Golden Flicker Reel flame with the bank-A knob at a quarter and Bloom and Trails lit so each moth leaves a ring of dust, then pull the knob to zero on the breakdown and the moths die in two bars.
- The Table to Class A: fire Scene Launch 4 and roll the bank-D knob up with Mirror Quad lit so one glass becomes four and the damask folds into a cathedral on the bass drop, then walk Up three times to Class A, pull faders 1 and 2 to zero so only the Static, the needle and the cold blue voice remain, press the Mirror button so the hiss folds into a face that was not there, and tap Invert RGB for one beat so the parlour goes negative and the name shows through.

**Hour Of Saturn**
- The Argento cut: column transition time is zero in this deck and the crossfader is never touched; fire Scene Launch 4 and Scene Launch 5 on alternate downbeats (Red Gel, Blue Gel) so the lamp swaps sides of the room on the bar, with the bank-D knob at a quarter and Kaleidoscope lit so the Kamea multiplies to four under the second gel and no further.
- Hour Struck: fire Scene Launch 2 and press the row-4 LIGHT pad once on the hit with React Anamorphic lit (Streak high, Squeeze up) so the hour sounds like a lens catching steel, never twice in a bar, while Static Two-tone on row 7 roughens the print for exactly that bar.
- Conjuration to FINE: walk Up to Conjuration, hold the row-2 BODY pad and the row-3 FIGURE pad together (Piano trigger) and push the bank-A knob to full with Bloom lit so the contracting rings pull into the seal until the frame whites out, drop the knob so the circle is empty, then walk Up twice through The Lamp Goes, where only the verdigris point and the text remain, to Licence To Go, where the row-5 pad reads LICENCE TO DEPART and, beneath it, FINE.

**German Bight**
