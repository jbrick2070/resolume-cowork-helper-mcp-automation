# Dead Air - Sume clip prompts

One loopable clip per scene, 40 in all, to sit under the generators as a photographic BED or AURA
layer. The APC40 preset does not change. Text stays on the TEXT layer, so no prompt asks for
lettering, and no prompt asks for a readable face, so nothing needs to match anything else.

## Settings (every clip)

| | |
|---|---|
| Model | Gemini Omni Flash 1.1 (4K, $0.40 at the 9:16 default; confirm the 16:9 price before the run) |
| Fallback | Wan 3.0 or MiniMax H3 if Omni Flash drifts or adds lettering |
| Length | 5 s (10 s for the five slow BED scenes marked *long*) |
| Aspect | 16:9 |
| Resolution | highest offered |
| Start frame | none; text to video throughout |

**Suffix appended to every prompt:** `Locked-off camera, no camera movement, slow continuous
motion that can loop, no people, no text, no captions, no logos, no lettering of any kind,
photographic, 1970s 16mm grain, very dark exposure with one light source.`

**Test set first (one per deck, about $2):** 1.3 Chart Recorder, 2.2 The Loop, 3.5 Moth To Lamp,
4.4 Red Gel, 5.1 Sailing By. Judge loopability and whether the model keeps lettering out before
spending on the other 35.

## Deck 1 - Hydrogen Line

Palette for the model: near-black #050608, buff paper #e8e6df, pen-lamp amber #d9a441, one red #b3162b.

1. **Dark Run** *(long)* - A radio telescope control hut at three in the morning, seen from the back of the room. Everything is sensor-bias grey. A single strip of chart paper creeps out of a recorder, lit only by a small gooseneck pen lamp. Dust hangs in the lamp beam. Nothing else moves.
2. **Scruff** - Extreme close-up of a chart recorder pen on buff paper. The pen draws a flat line, then a tiny quarter-inch burst of ink scruff, then flat again, over and over at a slow steady rhythm. Amber lamp light from the upper left, paper grain visible.
3. **Chart Recorder** - A 1970s strip chart recorder in a dark hut, paper feeding slowly left to right at one inch per minute, pen arm trembling. One pen lamp makes a pool of warm light on the paper; the rest of the frame falls to black.
4. **CP 1919** - A stack of thin white pulse profiles on black, each a jagged ridgeline, drawn one above the other like a mountain range, the lowest one pulsing faintly at a 1.3 second beat. Pure line on black, no colour.
5. **Wow Signal** - A fanfold computer printout on a desk under a pen lamp. Slowly, from the bottom of the frame up, a wash of deep hydrogen-alpha red rises across the paper over the whole clip, then holds. Film grain, the only red in the deck.
6. **Deep Field** *(long)* - A glass photographic plate developing in a tray of liquid in a darkroom. Faint smudges of galaxies emerge out of nothing across the whole plate, thousands of them, as the developer works. Dim deep-blue safelight, slow, no hands.
7. **No Carrier** - A dead P1 phosphor oscilloscope screen in a dark room, a flat horizontal trace with slight hum jitter, phosphor decay, faint static snow in the glass, the room reflected as almost nothing.
8. **Dawn Chorus** *(long)* - A large radio dish seen from below against a pre-dawn sky going from black to grey-blue. The dish mesh is a hexagonal lattice against the sky. A faint curl of high cloud drifts. Cold and quiet.

## Deck 2 - Loss Of Signal

Palette: console grey #0b0d0f, P39 phosphor green #1f8a55 and #35d07f, tally amber #e3a21a, far-side violet #5b3c8a.

1. **Go For TLI** - 1969 Mission Control console row from above, dark, a grid of unlit buttons with one amber tally lamp lit. Green phosphor glow from an unseen screen below the frame. Cigarette smoke drifts through the lamp.
2. **The Loop** - A green phosphor carrier wave on a black CRT, full width along the lower third of the frame, a clean steady sine with gentle breathing amplitude. Scanlines and glass reflection. Nothing above it but dark.
3. **LOS In Ten** - The lunar limb seen from an orbiting spacecraft window, a bright grey crescent edge sliding slowly toward the centre of the frame against black space, the terminator eating the light. No stars.
4. **Dead Air** *(long)* - Near total black. A single amber lamp in a dark console room pulses very slowly like a heartbeat, once every few seconds, lighting nothing but its own bezel. Film grain in the black.
5. **Whistling** - A corridor of concentric violet rings receding into darkness like the inside of a long tunnel, slowly pulling inward toward the viewer, thin lines on black, faint purple haze.
6. **Alarm 1202** - A 1960s spacecraft control panel in the dark, a red MASTER ALARM lamp flashing on and off at one second intervals, green phosphor readouts jittering with horizontal glitch tears. Tight shot, panel only.
7. **Acquisition** - Sunrise over the lunar horizon from orbit: a thin white line of light along the limb that swells into one long white flare across the whole frame, then settles to a sunlit grey surface. Lens flare, no stars.
8. **Splashdown** - 1969 black-and-white newsreel footage of a space capsule under three parachutes drifting down over an overcast grey sea, heavy film grain, scratches, soft focus, flat grey sky.

## Deck 3 - The Sitters

Palette: parlour black #120e0b, daguerreotype brown #6b4a2e, muslin grey #cfd6cc, candle #f2b65a, one cold blue #2b6cff.

1. **Lights Down** - A Victorian parlour lit by one candle on a round table. An oil lamp in the background is being turned down to nothing over the clip until only the candle remains. Sepia daguerreotype tones, heavy vignette, no people in frame.
2. **Join Hands** - Close-up of damask wallpaper and tablecloth in candlelight, the pattern faintly breathing as the flame moves. Warm brown and cream, vesica and acanthus damask repeat, slow flicker.
3. **Knock Once** - A candle flame on a dark table flinches hard once, as if struck, then recovers, then flinches again on a slow regular beat. Ripples spread across the water in a glass beside it. Sepia.
4. **The Table** - A heavy round seance table in candlelight tilts slowly to the left on its own, the candle and glass sliding a little, then settles. Smoke in the air, dark parlour, sepia.
5. **Moth To Lamp** - A single oil-lamp flame in a dark room with moths circling it, crossing the light again and again, scattering dust that glows and drifts. Warm amber on black, shallow focus.
6. **Trumpet Lifts** - A tin spirit trumpet on a dark table lifts slowly into the air by itself in candlelight, turning gently, the lamp shade behind it throwing faceted light on the wall. Sepia with a faint spiral glow.
7. **Class A** - A dark room with a single cold blue glow opening in the upper left corner, like a draught made visible, slowly growing and pulsing while everything else stays black. Wax cylinder phonograph horn faint in the shadow.
8. **Break Circle** - The candle goes out and the cold blue fades to full black over the clip; the last of a muslin curtain settles. Ends on black with film grain so the loop can restart from dark.

## Deck 4 - Hour Of Saturn

Palette: crypt black #0a0908, vellum #d8c9a8, verdigris #4f8a6b, Giallo red #c1121f, Giallo blue #1f4fbf. Gels cut hard, never fade.

1. **Chalk Circle** - A nine-foot chalk circle on a dark stone floor seen from above, lit hard from the left by a single lamp, chalk dust still settling. Sigils around the rim. The lamp does not move.
2. **Hour Struck** - A hexagonal sigil tile pattern on a stone wall, lit from one side, with a single hard flash of white light across it once per clip like a blade catching light. Grainy 1977 Italian film print.
3. **First Seal** - A three-by-three magic square scratched into verdigris copper, close up, the numerals unreadable, the metal corroding and brightening in slow pulses, a soft grid of registration marks over it.
4. **Red Gel** - A stone crypt corridor lit entirely in saturated Giallo red from the wrong side, the light cutting in hard like a stage gel. Dust in the beam, a hexagonal floor pattern, Suspiria lighting, no fade.
5. **Blue Gel** - The same crypt corridor lit entirely in saturated Giallo blue from the opposite side, with a pentagonal kaleidoscope pattern of light on the floor that flashes once. Hard light, no fade.
6. **Conjuration** - Concentric rings of light on black contracting slowly toward the centre of a seal, pulling inward and blooming brighter as they tighten until the centre almost whites out. Red and blue gels meeting at the edges of the frame.
7. **The Lamp Goes** - A single oil lamp in a crypt guttering and dying, its flame shrinking to a verdigris-green point, the red wash on the walls fading to ember. Grain heavy, print dying.
8. **Licence To Go** - An empty chalk circle on a stone floor in near black, only a faint ember glow on the wall, chalk dust drifting. Ends on black.

## Deck 5 - German Bight

Palette: night sea #0e141a, swell grey #5f6f78 and #8c979c, lamp white #ffe8a3, gale red #a8323a, bridge radar green.

1. **Sailing By** *(long)* - The North Sea at night as a slow heaving swell under an overcast sky, nearly monochrome blue-grey. A lighthouse on the horizon gives two white flashes every ten seconds, then nothing. The only motion is the sea and the light.
2. **Gale Warnings** - A storm sea at night, stacked breaking swells rolling toward the camera, spray in the air, a red navigation light reflecting on the water from the left edge, dark and violent but no horror cue.
3. **Viking Utsire** - Two lighthouses far apart on a black horizon, each pulsing white at its own rhythm, over a calm cold sea with a faint northern glow in the sky.
4. **Dogger Fisher** - Rain falling in straight lines through the beam of a ship's deck light at night, a few orange buoys drifting on a dark grey swell beyond, harbour lights blurred on the horizon.
5. **German Bight** - A lighthouse beam sweeping across a rough sea toward the camera, the wedge of light revealing the swell and then leaving it dark, hiss of spray, long-wave radio night.
6. **Lundy Fastnet** - The Fastnet rock lighthouse at night on a black sea, its flash lighting the wet rock and the arcs of swell breaking around it, green starboard light of a passing vessel at the right edge.
7. **Rockall Malin** - A single black rock in a bank of sea fog at night, a fog lamp making a soft blurred halo, swell barely visible, everything grey on grey. Very little motion.
8. **Fair Isle** *(long)* - A flat calm sea at first light, horizon line dead level, the sky going from black to the palest grey, a lighthouse flash growing weaker as the dawn comes up. Ends almost white-grey.

## Delivery

Download each clip named `DA_<deck>_<scene>_<slug>.mp4` (for example `DA_1_3_chart_recorder.mp4`)
into `packs/dead-air/media/`. The builder can then place them as a BED-under layer per scene;
that is a separate build step and not part of the prompt pass.
