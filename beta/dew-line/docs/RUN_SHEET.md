# Dew Line - run sheet

## Before the first frame

1. Install: `APC 40 MK II - Dew Line.xml` -> `Documents\Resolume Avenue\Shortcuts\MIDI\`; `Dew Line: The Spinningwork.avc` -> `Documents\Resolume Avenue\Compositions\` (this rig's Documents is under OneDrive).
2. **Audio in.** Preferences -> Audio -> Input device: `CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable)`. Play your music into `CABLE Input`. The composition uses External FFT; with no input device every reactive parameter sits at its floor and the picture looks static.
3. Preferences -> MIDI: `APC40 mkII` as input AND output (output drives the pad colours).
4. Open the composition. It selects its own MIDI preset.
5. Park the CROSSFADER in the centre and all eight faders up.

## Opening

* **PLAY** fires scene 1 of the current deck and arms the SCOPE.
* Watch the SCOPE (layer 8, fader 8): if it is flat, the audio input is not set.
* Track knobs 1-6 are depth / release for the three bands; turn depth up until the SCOPE breathes with the music, not with every hi-hat.
* **FFT GAIN is the TEMPO knob**, on every composition: Avenue's global audio gain. Turn it up when the picture is too calm for the room, down when it slams. METRONOME puts it back to the middle.
* **TIME is Track knob 8**: composition speed, 0 to 2x with 1x at the centre. TAP TEMPO puts it back to exactly 1x.

## Playing

* Scenes with Scene Launch 1-5 and UP/DOWN; worlds with LEFT/RIGHT.
* Pads mash the five playable layers across any scene.
* Effects: lit button = active. Device knobs 1-4 are the four banks' wetness, 5-8 the macros. CUE LEVEL nudges all four banks together (anticlockwise toward the clean picture).
* **STOP** clears the pad layers only (beds and scope survive). **STOP ALL CLIPS** ejects everything. **RECORD** is blackout (press again to return). The footswitch is a momentary blackout.
* **BANK LOCK = CLEAN.** Every effect dry, every macro off, picture untouched. Avenue has no soft-takeover: after CLEAN the physical knobs are where you left them, so the next knob you touch jumps. Sweep CUE LEVEL to come back smoothly.

## Signature moments, deck by deck

**Orb Plate 1889**
- Walk Up from Bridge Line to The Hub one scene per eight bars with the Bank A knob (Radar plus Trails) at half, so the sweep lays each stage of the orb rather than cutting it in; never jump to The Hub early, she does not.
- Formwork removal: hold Dry Spiral, then press the Capture Spiral column button and let the long transition run with Bank A Trails up, so the dense spiral eats the sparse one from rim to hub while the row-5 TEXT pad stamps SCAFFOLD REMOVED.
- Dew Load into Eaten at Dawn: hit the row-4 LIGHT pad so Metaballs bead every radius at once, ride the Bank C knob (Suckr on the kick) so the orb tightens toward the hub under the weight and comes back to zero with the geometry intact; then stop the row-2 BODY pad first and leave the row-5 TEXT pad lit for four bars so the words outlive the web.

**Sit and Wait**
- Sit in Twelve Days with only the row-1 BED and row-2 BODY pads lit and the SCOPE as the single moving thing for longer than feels safe, then fire Scene Launch 3 on a kick and let Line Scape Wobble on the highs say that something landed.
- On Pluck the Line, tap the row-4 Stroboscope pad by hand in a two-then-pause rhythm; the strobe is never clocked and never on the FFT, your finger is the spider's foreleg; at The Rush slam the Bank C knob (Displace) to full on the drop, press Fragment in Bank D once and let go, and pull Bank C back to zero within two bars so the sheet collapses and re-forms.
- The Husk: toggle Freeze in Bank D, then pull every layer fader except TEXT, GRAIN and SCOPE to zero; the room is left with one red word, static and the trace, and you stay there longer than the room expects.

**Arachne's Loom**
- Warp and Weft into Gods' Crimes: the two row-2 BODY pads are the shuttle, alternate them on eighth-notes so the Checkered weave flips under your fingers; then on Shuttle Thrown press the Bank A Kaleidoscope-6 button and, four bars later, the Kaleidoscope-12 button with the Bank A knob half open, so the tapestry folds into six then twelve and Athena arrives at twelve.
- The Verdict into Hang Forever: fire Scene Launch 4, hold the row-4 LIGHT pad (Stroboscope Madder, long Fade) while the Text Block reads VERDICT: HANG, press Fragment in Bank D once and let go so the cloth tears for one bar and heals, then fire Scene Launch 5 on the downbeat and the Shaper cuts from woman to octagon with no transition.
- Ananse Web: with the Bank A knob (Radar plus Trails) up, mash the row-5 TEXT pads of Story Nodes and Ananse Web so the four captures land on the web one by one as the beam passes each node; the Rings body is the Ananse cosmology diagram and the sweep is the storyteller.

**Goose Summer**
- Egg Sac into Three Hundred into The Gift: hold the row-3 FIGURE pad and raise the layer-3 knob slowly so the Metaballs Grid climbs from 4 to 20 behind the Line Scape silk while the Bank B knob (Bloom) warms the lantern; then on The Gift ride the Bank C knob (Suckr) so the small bodies are drawn inward to the mother Shaper on row 2, stop her pad, and leave the children.
- Moult into Ecdysis: fire Scene Launch 4, on the break fire 5 and punch Stop Motion in Bank D; tap the row-3 Shaper pad twice quickly so the retrigger leaves the outline hanging in Bank A Trails as the shed skin, and release Stop Motion exactly when she clears it.
- Balloon into Beagle Rigging: row-2 Lines (sixty hairline threads) with the Bank C knob (Displace, on highs) at half so they shiver, then fire Beagle Rigging and let the lighthouse Stroboscope on row 4 turn while you pull every fader but BED, TEXT and SCOPE down over eight bars; the threads let go one at a time into the morning.

**Dew Line Station**
- Carrier into Sweep: the Bank A Radar is locked to eight beats, one revolution per bar, each leg a beat; mash the row-2 BODY pads on the beat so a new ring lands exactly where the beam is, and the eight-legged clock keeps the room's time without a frame of media.
- Maman into Net of Light: fire Scene Launch 4 and the Steel octagon fills the wall from above; bring the Bank B knob (Bloom plus Sparkles) up over eight bars so every marble egg and every dew return grows a four-armed cross, her shadow on every star; on Eight O'Clock the CP1919 Stroboscope on row 4 ticks at 0.75 Hz against the eight-beat sweep, two clocks that do not agree.
- Blackout into Sign Off: hold a real black for a full phrase with only SCOPE and NO CONTACT 0300 in red, then walk the Test Card up, push the Bank B knob (CRT) to full so the tube collapses toward a dot under CLOSEDOWN, and stop the composition; no fade, like 1981 television.
