# Studio One - run sheet

## Before the first frame

1. Install: `APC 40 MK II - Studio One.xml` -> `Documents\Resolume Avenue\Shortcuts\MIDI\`; `Studio One - War Of The Worlds 1938.avc` -> `Documents\Resolume Avenue\Compositions\` (this rig's Documents is under OneDrive).
2. **Audio in.** Preferences -> Audio -> Input device: `CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable)`. Play your music into `CABLE Input`. The composition uses External FFT; with no input device every reactive parameter sits at its floor and the picture looks static.
3. Preferences -> MIDI: `APC40 mkII` as input AND output (output drives the pad colours).
4. Open the composition. It selects its own MIDI preset.
5. Park the CROSSFADER in the centre and all eight faders up.

## Opening

* **PLAY** fires scene 1 of the current deck and arms the SCOPE.
* Watch the SCOPE (layer 8, fader 8): if it is flat, the audio input is not set.
* Track knobs 1-6 are depth / release for the three bands; turn depth up until the SCOPE breathes with the music, not with every hi-hat.

## Playing

* Scenes with Scene Launch 1-5 and UP/DOWN; worlds with LEFT/RIGHT.
* Pads mash the five playable layers across any scene.
* Effects: lit button = active. Device knobs 1-4 are the four banks' wetness, 5-8 the macros. CUE LEVEL sweeps all four banks.
* **STOP** clears the pad layers only (beds and scope survive). **STOP ALL CLIPS** ejects everything. **RECORD** is blackout (press again to return). The footswitch is a momentary blackout.
* **BANK LOCK = CLEAN.** Every effect dry, every macro off, picture untouched. Avenue has no soft-takeover: after CLEAN the physical knobs are where you left them, so the next knob you touch jumps. Sweep CUE LEVEL to come back smoothly.

## Signature moments, deck by deck

**Studio One**
- Roll it: Scene Launch 2 on the downbeat, tap the row-5 TEXT pad so ROLL IT retypes, and hold the row-3 FIGURE pad so the lathe disc turns under Nora's arm.
- Foley: Scene Launch 4 and turn Device knob 6 (ECHO) up so the jar lid leaves rings in the iron pot; tap STUTTER on each scrape.
- Dead air: walk UP to column 8 on the loudest moment of the track, let the room freeze for a full phrase, then fire Scene Launch 1 to come back on air.

**Grovers Mill**
- The cap: Scene Launch 4, hold the row-3 FIGURE pad so the cap turns on the mids, and ride the Device knob 3 (SPACE) with TUNNEL lit to pull the cylinder's ribs inward.
- Heat ray: Scene Launch 5 then UP to Heat Ray on the drop; the beam's width follows the kick. Tap STROBE on the hit only, never twice in a bar.
- Field lost: UP to column 7 mid-phrase so the picture cuts to black under the strip, hold a full bar of nothing, then Scene Launch 1 of deck 1 to return to the studio.

**The Visitors**
- Red line: Scene Launch 6 with every effect off; hold the row-3 FIGURE pad so the red pencil line appears only while you hold it, and let it go on the downbeat.
- Station break: UP to column 7, pull fader 3 down to a third so the red line sits under Doyle's microphone like a thought he cannot finish.
- Clock held: column 8, push Track knob 8 (TIME) fully anticlockwise so every generator in the room stops with the sweep hand; bring it back to centre to release.

**New York**
- The dive: Scene Launch 3 on the build and push Track knob 8 (TIME) past three-quarters so the nose glazing rushes; drop it to centre on the impact.
- The window: UP from The Roof to The Window on the drop - smoke cuts to a wet, lit, untouched avenue. Hold it; that is the proof.
- Nineteen: column 7, Device knob 3 (SPACE) at a third with MIRROR QUAD lit so the row of machines multiplies; release on the line about the birds.

**Morning After**
- Doors: Scene Launch 1 on the last hit of a track; the uniforms' edges shiver on the mids. Tap STROBE once for the doors.
- Headlines to two percent: Scene Launch 4, hold it for a phrase, then UP to Two Percent so the front page collapses to one thin bar.
- The disc: walk UP to column 7, pull every fader but 2, 3 and 8, and let the strip carry the music out; then UP to Next Week and BLACKOUT on the last beat.
