APC40 mkII Colour Lab a MIDI router proof of concept

A pad's MIDI velocity picks its colour from a fixed 128-entry palette and its MIDI channel picks an animation (static, one-shot, pulsing, blinking) that the controller then runs by itself. A router between the APC40 and Resolume can therefore re-colour any pack's feedback, fill the dark pads with an ambient show and answer every press, using two MIDI messages per pad. Click the pads.

Flashing content: pads blink and pulse. Use Pause at any time. This page emulates the LED protocol from the Akai APC40 Mk2 Communications Protocol v1.2 and Ableton's own APC40 mkII skin; the animation timings have not yet been confirmed on this rig's hardware.

    How it works

    ChannelAnimationRates (MIDI clock ticks at 24 per quarter note)
    1primary colourstatic
    2 to 6one-shot to the secondary colour1/24 · 1/16 · 1/8 · 1/4 · 1/2 (4 · 6 · 12 · 24 · 48 ticks)
    7 to 11pulsing between primary and secondarysame five rates
    12 to 16blinking between primary and secondarysame five rates
    • Recipe. Send the primary colour on channel 1, then the secondary on an animation channel. Sending a primary again on channel 1 cancels the animation.
    • Router. Resolume keeps the APC's input for presses; the router sits on the LED path. It re-colours the feedback through a theme (green becomes pumpkin, or white), fills pads Resolume leaves dark, and answers each press with a ripple, a cross, a sparkle or an inversion, all as one-shot messages timed by the controller.
    • Mode. The controller starts in Mode 0 after every power cycle. Send F0 47 7F 29 60 00 04 42 00 00 00 F7 (Mode 2) so the host owns every LED, rings included.
    • Not yet confirmed on hardware. That the pads follow a host MIDI clock (and the tempo they use without one), the exact one-shot direction, and whether the Scene Launch pads animate like the clip pads. The demo draws them as the documents describe.
    Pad colours: the Akai APC40 mkII RGB palette from Akai's APC40 Mk2 Communications Protocol v1.2 (display approximations). Engine: apc_lab (Python) and demo/engine.js, checked against each other on 54 scenarios.
